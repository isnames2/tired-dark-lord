A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — an entry for the make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue".

Bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb

## The monologue

> The little knight stands before me, sword up, all fire. I like fire. It saves me the candles. The rite is set, the moon is right, my ravens did their part, and by dawn my flags will fly from here to the Grey Sea. Kneel. Kneel, and I shall be kind — once the books are done. The books! Nine towns owe me grain, four owe me gold, and the quill weighs more than my sword. Sit. Drink. The dark can wait one night. It has waited a thousand years. Tomorrow, hero. Tomorrow I end your world. Tonight… I sit.

Exactly 100 words, 500 characters. The tiredness lives in the deflections — the candles he won't light, the quill heavier than his sword, the eclipse postponed one more night — never in a plain statement. He still has his rite, his ravens, and his flags over the Grey Sea.

## Run

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
